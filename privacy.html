JJ Bot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

JJ Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jeremy Elias. It has no other users.

What it accesses

JJ Bot reads only the owner's own YouTube channel analytics and channel data, using read-only YouTube and YouTube Analytics permissions. It does not post, edit, or delete anything.

Where the data goes

The data stays in the owner's personal tools (his assistant workspace and his Notion workspace). It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
